Photo location test

Checks whether photos picked inside Telegram still carry their GPS tags. Everything runs on your phone. No photos are uploaded.
Before you pick
  1. iPhone: in the photo picker, tap Options at the top and turn Location on if you see it.
  2. Pick as many photos as you like (700+ is fine). If the picker gets stuck, pick in batches: results add up, and repeats are skipped.
  3. Screenshot the result and send it to me.
